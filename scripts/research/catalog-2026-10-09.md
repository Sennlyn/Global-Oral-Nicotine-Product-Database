# Catalog expansion — 2026-10-09

## Result and coverage

570 records, 74 brands, 66 historical records; 489 entries added to the existing 81.
All existing IDs, product photographs and URLs are retained. The baseline IDs are
recorded in `catalog-baseline-2026-10-09.json` against main commit
`7b60230a90d7e97220eec5ec9e592780fb0d2a6f`.

This is a dated research batch, not a claim of global or historical completeness.
An accessible listing, scientific sample or regulatory mention can establish
existence without establishing present legal availability or a complete SKU.

## Sources examined

- VELO: UK all-cans catalog; USA original, PLUS, MAX and FAQ; Pakistan official
  flavor catalog and strength guide. Regional compositions remain separate.
- Northerner US: on! mini dry, Rogue and Rogue Max, Lucy Slim and Breakers,
  zone, FRE, ALP and Grizzly tobacco-leaf-free nicotine pouch directories.
- Lucy: official pouch, Breakers and gum product pages. REVIVE caffeine gum
  is outside the nicotine catalog.
- Nordic Spirit: UK official standard/mini catalog, pages 1 and 2. Named
  strength classes remain textual when the numeric variant cannot be established.
- Snusdirect: LOOP and KLINT directories, including temporarily unavailable
  and not-for-sale entries. Indexed references also establish the named ACE,
  Skruf Superwhite, Après and Thor entries. Sidebar brand names do not establish
  a product identity and are not converted into invented products.
- Nicorette UK: exact named gum/lozenge strengths and pack sizes. Existing
  2 mg / 40-piece and 105-piece entries are preserved. A 4 mg mention is not
  assigned the pack size of a 2 mg variant.
- Nicotinell: named emc medicine records; oral gum and lozenges only.
- HPRA Ireland: NiQuitin Strips Mint 2.5 mg in the withdrawn medicine directory.
  Withdrawal in that market is not treated as proof of global discontinuation.
- Oliver Twist: official UK and USA tobacco-bit assortments. Overlapping
  named flavors are merged into one record with both market sources.
- Pixotine: official nicotine toothpick collection; bulk cartons are not
  counted as different products, and toothpicks are not labeled dissolving sticks.
- Stepanov et al. (2008): named Marlboro Snus, Camel Snus, Skoal Dry and
  Taboka samples. Historical laboratory values are not copied into current packs.
- FDA NSE/enforcement records (2014–2015): named Ariva variants.
- Star Scientific's 2010 Form 10-K: Stonewall Natural and Wintergreen.
- Nikam et al. (2024), table 1, via the Healis publisher PDF: 40 named
  tobacco-containing plain chewing tobacco, khaini, gutkha/gutkha-like and
  companion-tobacco entries. Samples were purchased August–September 2019.
  Publication year is not the purchase date. Only the companion tobacco is
  represented in pan-masala records; plain tobacco-free pan masala is excluded.

Actual URLs, source classifications, access dates and evidence notes are stored
with the records in `src/data/catalog-expansion.ts` and shown on product pages.

## Deduplication and uncertainty

- Multiple retailers, countries, quantity discounts, mixed bundles and new
  artwork do not automatically create another product.
- LOOP Cassis Bliss Strong is a former name of Blackcurrant Strong. It is
  retained as a search and URL alias, with rename evidence. Reported recipe and
  numeric strength changes are not silently collapsed into a fabricated dose.
- VELO Pakistan's Music Edition artwork is provenance, not another flavor.
  Identical names/strengths found in both UK and Pakistan share sources and
  markets. Unknown production sites are not inferred from another market.
- US VELO, PLUS and MAX are separate series. The current 8/12 mg MAX is not
  silently conflated with earlier retail uses of the MAX name for 7 mg products.
- `verificationStatus: pending` applies to all new entries because exact
  variant photos and/or additional fields are incomplete. Source-backed identity
  does not bypass the existing full-verification image requirement.
- Stock shortages do not establish discontinuation. Historical identity, active
  lifecycle, full verification and country-specific authorization are independent.
- Unknown manufacturer, origin, numerical strength, unit mass, flavor intensity,
  pH and image remain unknown. No generated packaging or substitute photos.

## Validation

The audit checks all old IDs, product/brand/manufacturer/taxonomy/market joins,
source completeness, duplicate IDs/names/slugs, full verification rules, historical
context, image provenance, aliases and protection against unrelated brand-wide
specification enrichment. The full website is built with the GitHub Pages base
path to validate every generated product, brand and market route.
