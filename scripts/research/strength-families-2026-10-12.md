# Strength-family corrections — 12 October 2026

The ten classic U.S. ZYN flavors were displayed as separate 1.5 mg and 3/6 mg cards because only the 3/6 mg records carried a documented nicotine-origin value. The grouping boundary correctly distinguishes nicotine sources, but the incomplete 1.5 mg metadata created a false split.

[PMI's official oral-products FAQ](https://www.pmi.com/faq-section/nicotine-pouches-snus-oral-smokeless-products), checked on 12 October 2026, explicitly identifies ZYN nicotine as extracted from the tobacco plant. The classic-product builder now records tobacco-derived origin with this source for all its strengths. [PMI U.S.'s portfolio release](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/pmi-us-expands-zyn-portfolio-to-offer-adults-more-smoke-free-choices-302874306.html) also places 1.5, 3 and 6 mg in the same flagship dry-pouch lineup and distinguishes the higher-moisture ULTRA range. No physical measurements or authorization are inferred for 1.5 mg from the 3/6 mg records.

The existing Skruf Superwhite no.54 Fresh Mint Xtra Strong record also lacked the Superwhite series designation carried by no.52/53. The series is explicit in its recorded name and linked [product reference](https://snuskompagniet.com/products/skruf-super-white-slim-fresh-stark-4). Normalize this series and retain the full Xtra Strong button label. The remaining VELO lookalikes belong to separately recorded UK and Pakistan ranges and retain their region distinctions.

All 747 product records, IDs, source links and individual pages remain. Presentation families decrease from 418 to 407 (ZYN from 32 to 22). Buttons continue to select the exact dose's image and detail page. Mini, ULTRA, capsule, historical and different-series boundaries remain intact.

Related-product grids inside brand/category/format/market content panels use a single column on screens up to 700 px. This prevents the brand-page strength controls and package images from being squeezed into three narrow columns.
