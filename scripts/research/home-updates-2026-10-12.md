# Homepage sources checked on 12 October 2026

The homepage now presents five official updates and 22 chronological milestones. Bilingual text and per-event links are maintained in `src/data/home-updates.ts`. Product records and market status are unchanged by this editorial update.

## Recent updates

- [U.S. GAO report GAO-26-108195](https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-26-108195): published 30 September, publicly released 1 October 2026. The card uses the public release date and states both dates. Its findings concern tobacco application reviews overall, not just nicotine pouches; it is not a marketing order.
- [FDA registration and product listing form update](https://www.fda.gov/tobacco-products/ctp-newsroom/fda-updates-tobacco-product-establishment-registration-and-product-listing-form-increase-efficiency): announcement 29 September, form launched 28 September 2026. Includes smokeless tobacco and oral nicotine products. Registration/listing is distinguished from marketing authorization.
- [FDA ZYN ULTRA orders](https://www.fda.gov/tobacco-products/ctp-newsroom/fda-authorizes-11-new-nicotine-pouches): 21 August 2026, ten 9 mg specifications and Smooth 11 mg.
- [FDA on! orders](https://www.fda.gov/tobacco-products/ctp-newsroom/pilot-program-update-fda-authorizes-4-new-nicotine-pouches): 4 August 2026, four named flavor/strength combinations.
- [FDA ZYN modified-risk orders](https://www.fda.gov/tobacco-products/ctp-newsroom/fda-authorizes-20-zyn-nicotine-pouches-be-marketed-specific-modified-risk-claim): 30 June 2026, 20 specified 3/6 mg products and specified claims.

[FDA CTP newsroom](https://www.fda.gov/tobacco-products/ctp-newsroom) and [GAO](https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-26-108195) were checked. News reporting about possible accelerated approval policies was not treated as an enacted policy or new product authorization.

## Historical evidence

- Ettan 1822 and portion snus 1973 retain the existing Swedish Match [2005](https://www.swedishmatch.com/globalassets/reports/annual-reports/2005_annualreport_en.pdf) and [2021](https://www.swedishmatch.com/globalassets/reports/annual-reports/2021_swedishmatchannualreport_interactive_en.pdf) annual report sources. The 1973 date is also indexed in the company's [2017 presentation](https://www.swedishmatch.com/globalassets/documents/presentations/2017_q1_interimpresentation_swedishmatch_en.pdf).
- [General's manufacturer history](https://www.mynewsdesk.com/se/swedishmatch/pressreleases/general-787855) supports the 1866 recipe date.
- [Swedish Match's round-can anniversary release](https://news.cision.com/swedish-match/r/congratulations---the-round-snus-can-is-turning-40-,c368712) supports 1915 packaging standardization and 1967 round paper cans with metal lids.
- [Göteborgs Rapé anniversary release](https://www.mynewsdesk.com/se/swedishmatch/pressreleases/bubblig-goeteborgare-fyller-100-aar-ny-xr-goeteborgs-rape-sparkling-2860870) supports the 1919 brand and 1998 White product. White Portion is not a tobacco-leaf-free pouch.
- [Nicorette UK history](https://www.nicorette.co.uk/about-nicorette) supports the 1967 gum concept, 1978 Swiss launch and 1980 UK medicine registration. Medicinal nicotine replacement is labelled separately.
- [Catch manufacturer release](https://www.mynewsdesk.com/se/swedishmatch/pressreleases/foersta-smaksatta-snuset-stoersta-smaksatta-snusvarumaerket-1062959) supports the 1984 Licorice launch. The first-product claim is limited to the manufacturer-described Swedish portion-snus context.
- [Swedish Match business profile](https://www.swedishmatch.com/Our-business/) supports GOTHIATEK since 2000; this is a company quality standard.
- [ZYN's official history](https://www.zyn.com/gb/en/blog/when-were-nicotine-pouches-invented.html) supports its 2014 initial Colorado launch and 2016 Swedish entry. Its broader assertions about the invention of pouches are not reproduced as universal historical facts.
- [FDA General snus application history](https://www.fda.gov/tobacco-products/advertising-and-promotion/swedish-match-usa-inc-modified-risk-tobacco-product-mrtp-applications-general-snus-products) supports PMTA orders on 10 November 2015, MRTP orders on 22 October 2019 and renewal on 7 November 2024 until 7 November 2032.
- [FDA non-tobacco nicotine notice](https://www.fda.gov/tobacco-products/ctp-newsroom/requirements-products-made-non-tobacco-nicotine-take-effect-april-14) supports the 14 April 2022 effective date.
- [FDA first ZYN pouch orders](https://www.fda.gov/news-events/press-announcements/fda-authorizes-marketing-20-zyn-nicotine-pouch-products-after-extensive-scientific-review) supports 16 January 2025 and its exact 20-product scope. The later 2026 MRTP and PMTA milestones use the notices above.

Exact days are included only where documented. Multiple events in the same year have distinct IDs. The timeline is a selection of documented milestones rather than a complete global chronology.
